Features

Every plant. Every detail. One app.

From watering reminders to AR-powered light measurement, HousePlants gives you everything a serious plant parent needs — and nothing they don't.

💧

Smart Watering

Species-aware schedules that adapt to pot size, season, and your home's climate. Bulk water with one tap.

🔥

Streaks & Habits

Daily care streaks with animated flame UI, milestone confetti, and a satisfying loop that keeps plants alive.

🩺

Plant Doctor

Symptom-based diagnostic wizard. Identifies pests, deficiencies, and rot — then walks you through the fix.

📓

Plant Journal

Photo timelines, health scoring, and notes per plant. See your jungle grow over weeks and months.

⌚

Watch + Widgets

A companion Apple Watch app and home-screen widgets so today's care list is always one glance away.

🔒

Private by Design

Offline-first. Your collection, photos, and notes stay on your device. No accounts, no tracking.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is HousePlants free?+

Core features are free. A few advanced tools are part of an optional one-time upgrade.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. The entire catalog and all calculators run on-device. No account required.

Which iOS version?+

iOS 17.0 and up. Also runs as a companion app on Apple Watch.

Android?+

Not yet — built natively in SwiftUI to feel like a first-class iOS app.

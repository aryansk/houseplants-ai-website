Care guide

☀ Light

Wants strong light — a south or west window, or 6+ hours of direct sun. Acclimate gradually to avoid scorch.

💧 Water

Let the top inch of soil dry between waterings — typically about once a week.

🌡 Temperature

Comfortable indoors or out between roughly 15–29 °C. Move it inside before frost and away from cold drafts.

🪴 Repotting

Every 1–2 years, sizing up by one pot diameter. Refresh the soil annually to replenish nutrients.

🐾 Safety

Generally considered safe around cats, dogs, and children — though no plant should be eaten. A safe pick for pet households.

🌱 Propagation

Most members of Plectranthus propagate by stem cuttings or division. See the in-app Propagation Station for step-by-step guides.

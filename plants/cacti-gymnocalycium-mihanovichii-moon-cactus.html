Care guide

☀ Light

Wants strong light — a south or west window, or 6+ hours of direct sun. Acclimate gradually to avoid scorch.

💧 Water

Water sparingly — let the soil dry out completely, then a little more. Overwatering is the main risk.

🌡 Temperature

Comfortable indoors or out between roughly 15–29 °C. Move it inside before frost and away from cold drafts.

🪴 Repotting

Every 1–2 years, sizing up by one pot diameter. Refresh the soil annually to replenish nutrients.

🐾 Safety

Low toxicity — mild stomach upset is possible if eaten, but it's not considered dangerous. Still best kept out of reach of curious pets.

🌱 Propagation

Most members of Cacti propagate by stem cuttings or division. See the in-app Propagation Station for step-by-step guides.

Privacy

Last updated: June 2026

HousePlants is offline-first. Your plant collection, photos, notes, and care history live on your device — we don't have servers that hold them.

Data we don't collect

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics SDKs, no third-party tracking.
  • No advertising IDs.

Permissions we ask for

  • Camera — for the AR Sun Seeker and plant photo capture. Frames are processed on-device and never transmitted.
  • Location (optional) — for Climate Matcher's auto-detect. Stored only on your device. Manual entry is always available.
  • Notifications — for watering reminders. Scheduled locally on your device.

iCloud sync

If you enable it, your plant collection syncs through your personal iCloud account using CloudKit's private database. Only you (and Apple's infrastructure) can read it.
